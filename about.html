About Khaya

Rooted in the community we serve

Khaya means "home" — and that's exactly what we try to give every animal that comes through our gates, for as long as it takes to find them a family.

Our history

Khaya Animal Shelter was founded in 2017 by a small group of Benoni residents who were feeding stray dogs in their own neighbourhood and quickly realised the need was bigger than any one household could manage. What started as a backyard operation has grown into a registered non-profit shelter caring for dozens of animals at any given time.

Today we work with local vets, schools and community groups to rescue, treat, sterilise and rehome animals across Benoni and the wider East Rand.

Mission

To rescue abandoned, injured and neglected animals, restore them to health, and place them in safe, permanent homes — while educating our community on responsible pet ownership.

Vision

A community where no animal is left to fend for itself, and every pet has a home, food and a caring family.

Our values

  • Compassion firstEvery animal is treated with patience and care, no matter how long it takes to heal or find a home.
  • No animal turned awayWe never euthanise for space. If we're full, we find foster care rather than close our doors.
  • Community responsibilityWe run sterilisation drives and pet-care workshops so fewer animals need rescue in the first place.
