Donate

Khaya Animal Shelter runs almost entirely on donations. Here's how you can give:

  • Once-off donation — any amount helps cover vet bills, food or supplies.
  • Monthly giving — predictable income to plan ahead for vet care.
  • In-kind donations — food, blankets, towels, leashes and cleaning supplies.

Volunteer

Khaya Animal Shelter runs on volunteer hours as much as donations. Roles available:

  • Dog walking & socialising
  • Shelter upkeep (feeding, cleaning, maintenance)
  • Events & admin (adoption days, photography, social media)
